How to get to Kythnos

The island is reached by ferry. Book early — July is high season.

Ferry from Lavrio

The shortest crossing, to the port of Merichas. Lavrio is about an hour’s drive from Athens airport. Add your preferred operator and times here.

Ferry from Piraeus

Longer, but easy to reach by metro from central Athens. Check the summer timetable and book your tickets in advance.

On the island

Merichas is the main port. Hire a car or scooter, take the local bus or a taxi. We can help arrange transfers — just ask.

Where to do nothing, professionally

Kolona is the famous twin-bay beach: a sandbar with sea on both sides, reached by water taxi from Merichas or on foot. Apokrousi is one of the biggest, Episkopi is livelier, Loutra is easy for families and Agios Sostis is wilder and quieter. The island has dozens of beaches.

For when you feel guilty about doing nothing

Wander the traditional villages of Chora and Dryopida, then visit Katafyki Cave near Dryopida, one of the largest in Greece. Hike up to the medieval Castle of Oria for the view. Loutra is the only thermal spa village in the Cyclades, so don’t miss a soak in the hot springs.

For serious foodies

Tavernas line the waterfronts at Merichas and Loutra, and you’ll find more traditional places in Chora and Dryopida. Try fresh fish and local products. Our own favourites — add here.

No scooters after the party

Buses connect Merichas with Loutra, Dryopida and Kanala, and there are taxis and water taxis. A car or scooter gives the most freedom, as some beaches are reached by dirt tracks. Other islands such as Kea are a ferry ride away.

Opening times and ferry schedules change, so check before you go.
